# Investigation 02 — Oil under constraint

Research cutoff: 2026-09-22. User selected oil as Nexus's first full industry brief.

The observed briefing window is 2025-Q3 through 2026-Q2. June 2026 is the latest common endpoint across the selected EIA quarterly analysis, JODI update and China NBS release. Selected developments continue through 2026-09-10, with estimates, forecasts and policy announcements labelled separately.

This first pack focuses on the explanatory spine: global supply, Hormuz, alternate routes through Saudi and UAE infrastructure, Bab el-Mandeb, Suez/SUMED and the Cape route, alternatives around Malacca, China's import/refining response, U.S. production/exports and an OPEC+ decision. It does not trace individual cargoes, establish a single cause for prices, convert tonnes to barrels without an explicit conversion basis, or treat production requirements as actual output.

The fixture contains 13 reviewed public source records, 21 entities, 22 source-checked candidate assertions, 6 developments, 6 metric groups and 11 authored acceptance cases. Most quantitative claims rely on EIA analysis. JODI is retained to establish the common data cutoff but its bulk dataset has not yet been integrated. Wider source diversity and geographic geometry are the next research steps.

All records remain candidates until reviewed in Nexus. Passing schema or UI checks does not independently verify the underlying reports.
